Sipping sustainably.

Garage Sale Bar blends vintage fashion, nightlife and community into one experience. Shop the racks with a drink in your hand.

10%

Only 10% of clothing donated to thrift stores is actually sold.

We want to help change that. Garage Sale Bar exists to get people thinking about fast fashion and the waste it leaves behind. Every piece you buy vintage stays in use longer and cuts down on that waste.
